# Personas

1. **Mai the Busy Office Worker**

- Age 32
- Has a minor pre-existing condition (mild hypertension), limited free time, comfortable using mobile apps
- Wants: personalized health recommendations and quick appointment booking without phone calls

2. **Dr. Hung the Specialist Physician**

- Age 45
- Tight daily schedule, needs to anticipate patient volume in advance
- Wants: accurate appointment forecasting to reduce overload or being caught off guard

3. **Lan the Hospital Operations Manager**

- Age 38
- Responsible for allocating staff, examination rooms, and equipment
- Wants: real-time patient demand forecasting to plan resources effectively

4. **Mr. Tam the Elderly Patient**

- Age 68
- Low Tech Literacy
- Has multiple chronic conditions, often has questions outside office hours
- Wants: quick, simple consultation without waiting on hold with a call center

5. **Ms. Huong the Middle-Aged Patient**

- Age 55
- Has Chronic Illness (Type II Diabetes)
- Needs regular monitoring of blood sugar and blood pressure at home
- Wants: to avoid frequent hospital visits just for routine check-ups, but still feel safe knowing doctors will be alerted if something goes wrong
